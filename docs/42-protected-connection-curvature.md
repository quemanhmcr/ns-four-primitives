# Protected Connection Curvature: the Source as Covariant Work

**Claim level:** exact operator identities along every smooth periodic Navier-Stokes solution. This note is the positive result produced by the stochastic-Lagrangian falsification test.

The main observation is that the nonlinear Euler forcing can be written as a time-dependent **skew-adjoint connection** on velocity space. The optimal protected source is then exactly the curvature work generated by the failure of the moving protected multiplier to be parallel under that connection.

This formulation points toward hypocoercive/commutator geometry rather than raw martingale variance. Compare the general philosophy of Villani's hypocoercivity memoir, arXiv:math/0609050, while noting that the present operator is nonlinear and state-dependent.

## 1. A skew-dissipative form of Navier-Stokes

For the current vorticity `omega=curl u`, define on divergence-free vector fields

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_\omega v:=P(v\times\omega).
}
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal A_\omega u=P(u\times\omega)=F.
\]

Moreover `A_omega` is skew-adjoint in `L^2`:

\[
\boxed{
\langle v,\mathcal A_\omega w\rangle
=-\langle\mathcal A_\omega v,w\rangle.
}
\]

Indeed Leray projection is self-adjoint on divergence-free test fields and pointwise cross-product with `omega` is skew-symmetric.

Therefore the exact Navier-Stokes trajectory can be written

\[
\boxed{
\partial_tu=L_tu,
\qquad
L_t:=\mathcal A_{\omega(t)}-\nu\Lambda^2.
}
\]

This is linear in the transported argument once the actual solution `u(t)` is fixed, but its generator is state-dependent through `omega(t)`.

## 2. Moving protected multiplier

Let

\[
T_t=\Lambda-a(t)-b(t)\operatorname{curl},
\qquad
r=T_tu.
\]

`T_t` is self-adjoint and commutes with `Lambda^2`. Define its covariant curvature relative to the skew Euler connection by

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak K_t
:=\dot T_t+[T_t,\mathcal A_{\omega(t)}].
}
\]

Because `T_t` commutes with viscosity,

\[
\dot T_t+[T_t,L_t]
=\mathfrak K_t.
\]

Differentiate `r=T_tu`:

\[
\begin{aligned}
r_t
&=\dot T_tu+T_tL_tu\\
&=L_tr+
\bigl(\dot T_t+[T_t,L_t]\bigr)u.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence the exact covariant defect equation is

\[
\boxed{
 r_t
=(\mathcal A_\omega-\nu\Lambda^2)r
+\mathfrak K_tu.
}
\]

Viscosity damps the residual but contributes **zero curvature**. All creation of protected defect occurs through `K_t`.

## 3. Exact identification of the open source

Take the `L^2` product with `r`. Skew-adjointness gives

\[
\langle r,\mathcal A_\omega r\rangle=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac12\mathcal Y'
=\langle r,\mathfrak K_tu\rangle
-\nu\|\Lambda r\|_2^2.
\]

But the already established optimal-defect balance is

\[
\frac12\mathcal Y'
=\Gamma_{\rm esc}
-\nu\|\Lambda r\|_2^2.
\]

Thus, identically along the full nonlinear solution,

\[
\boxed{
\Gamma_{\rm esc}
=\langle r,\mathfrak K_tu\rangle.
}
\]

This is not a bound or analogy. The unresolved positive regeneration source is exactly **curvature work**.

## 4. Protected reset

At a nondegenerate protected reset,

\[
r(t_0)=0.
\]

Optimal stationarity gives

\[
a'(t_0)=b'(t_0)=0,
\]

so `dot T(t_0) u(t_0)=0`. Since `T u=0`,

\[
\mathfrak K_{t_0}u
=T\mathcal A_\omega u
=T F.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
 r_t(t_0)=\mathfrak K_{t_0}u(t_0)=TF(t_0),
}
\]

and the protected opening acceleration becomes

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal Y''(t_0)
=2\|\mathfrak K_{t_0}u(t_0)\|_2^2.
}
\]

Thus the reset trichotomy has an operator-geometric interpretation: a reset opens exactly when the protected subspace has nonzero curvature relative to the Euler connection.

## 5. Propagator commutator and holonomy

Let `U(t,s)` be the evolution operator for the non-autonomous linear equation along the fixed solution,

\[
\partial_tU(t,s)=L_tU(t,s),
\qquad U(s,s)=I.
\]

Define the protected transport commutator

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C(t,s)
:=T_tU(t,s)-U(t,s)T_s.
}
\]

It measures the failure of the protected multiplier to be parallel transported by the exact Navier-Stokes connection.

For `s<q<t`, direct composition gives the exact cocycle law

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C(t,s)
=\mathcal C(t,q)U(q,s)
+U(t,q)\mathcal C(q,s).
}
\]

Furthermore, differentiate

\[
U(t,\tau)T_\tau U(\tau,s)
\]

with respect to `tau`. Since

\[
\partial_\tau U(t,\tau)=-U(t,\tau)L_\tau,
\qquad
\partial_\tau U(\tau,s)=L_\tau U(\tau,s),
\]

one obtains the exact Duhamel-curvature formula

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C(t,s)
=
\int_s^t
U(t,\tau)\,
\mathfrak K_\tau\,
U(\tau,s)\,d\tau.
}
\]

So accumulated protected leakage is literally an integrated curvature/holonomy of the moving protection geometry.

## 6. Why this is more than notation

The old source problem was

\[
\Gamma_{\rm esc}
=\langle r,TF\rangle
\]

plus a complicated full-convolution interpretation of `TF`.

The new formulation is

\[
\boxed{
\text{state transport }L_t
=\text{skew Euler connection}
+\text{coercive viscosity},
}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\text{protected source}
=\text{work of the connection curvature }\mathfrak K_t.
}
\]

This exposes three structural facts simultaneously:

1. viscosity is exactly curvature-free because `[T,Lambda^2]=0`;
2. Euler transport is exactly skew in the ambient `L^2` metric;
3. defect regeneration is an off-diagonal commutator phenomenon.

Those are the canonical ingredients of hypocoercive and commutator-based geometry: a skew transport redistributes directions while a dissipative operator damps them, and mixed Lyapunov functionals are built from their bracket chain.

## 7. The new theorem target

The original No-Zeno/source gap can now be reformulated as:

> Can the curvature work `(<r,K_t u>)_+` have infinite total action while the skew-dissipative connection remains generated by one Navier-Stokes trajectory and while all exact Composition constraints hold?

A genuine hypocoercive closure would seek a scale-normalized modified energy

\[
\mathscr E
=\|r\|^2
+\epsilon_1\langle r,\mathfrak K u\rangle
+\epsilon_2\|\mathfrak K u\|^2
+\cdots
\]

or its RG-normalized counterpart, so that differentiation transfers viscous coercivity through the commutator chain and absorbs positive curvature work.

The important point is that this target is now an **operator bracket problem**, not a request for a stronger estimate on an arbitrary cubic term.

## 8. Falsification criteria for the curvature program

This candidate should be abandoned if any of the following occurs on normalized finite Galerkin packets:

1. the commutator chain `r, K u, D_t(Ku), ...` has arbitrarily long nearly-null directions while positive critical transfer remains order one;
2. every mixed hypocoercive functional necessarily loses a positive power of the active frequency scale;
3. curvature work can remain positive on a recurrent normalized orbit while every available commutator/coherence defect tends to zero.

Conversely, a scale-uniform finite-packet coercivity inequality would be a genuine opening because the full PDE gap has already been reduced to infinite positive curvature work.

## 9. Relation back to stochastic Lagrangian geometry

Constantin--Iyer supplies a stochastic **material** representation of viscous Navier--Stokes transport, but the first stochastic-variance test shows that raw martingale quadratic variation reproduces the dissipative side rather than the curvature source.

A crucial distinction must be kept: the propagator `U(t,s)` in this note is generated by the velocity-space operator

\[
L_t=\mathcal A_{\omega(t)}-\nu\Lambda^2,
\]

whereas the Constantin--Iyer stochastic Cauchy/Weber transport is a material-flow representation with deformation gradients. They are not the same propagator, and no equality of their commutator cocycles is claimed.

The stochastic test is nevertheless useful because it falsifies the naive variance route and points to the correct deterministic obstruction: **noncommutation of protection with transport**. A future stochastic theory-transfer would have to derive, rather than assume, a path-space representation of this curvature work or an equivalent material commutator.

Thus the immediate target is deterministic and exact: establish scale-uniform coercivity for the curvature/bracket chain. Only after that should stochastic path-space variance be reintroduced as a possible additional positive budget.
